Classroom Facilities Assistance Program Status
                                         By CFAP Status-Equity (as of December 31, 2025- FY26 Q2)

The Classroom Facilities Assistance Program (CFAP) was established in 1997 and is the main school facilities
program administered by OFCC. There is also the Exceptional Needs Program (ENP) for districts in signiﬁcant
need of resources immediately prior to their participation in CFAP as well as the Expedited Local Partnership
Program (ELPP), which allows for a district to complete a portion of their Master Facilities Plan prior to their
participation in CFAP and receive credit for that portion when they do participate in CFAP.
A district’s Master Facilities Plan is an essential guiding document for districts that lays out their entire plan.
Districts work with OFCC years ahead of construction to develop the plan that best suits the district’s needs.
OFCC looks at enrollment statistics, population projections, and educational needs when developing MFPs.
Participation in all OFCC school programs is based on the three-year average “adjusted valuation per pupil.”
This metric is calculated yearly by the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce using data from the
previous two years and the current year. It takes into consideration a district’s taxable property, district average
income, average daily membership, and the state median income for weighting.
This calculation is then used to rank districts in order lowest to highest. OFCC uses this ranking to make oﬀers
to districts starting with the lowest equity rank and working through the highest equity rank. This ranking is
also used to determine the state/local share of a project. For instance, the school at ranking 307 would have a
state/local split of 50/50. This is done by taking the ranking divided by the total number of schools which gives
you the local share.
