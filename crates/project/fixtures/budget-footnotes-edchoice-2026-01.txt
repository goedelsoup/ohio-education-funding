Number of School Buildings Designated for Traditional
EdChoice Increases for the 2026-2027 School Year
– Patrick Campbell, Budget Analyst

        In November 2025, DEW released the list of 513 public school buildings across 78
districts designated for the traditional EdChoice Scholarship Program for the 2026-2027 school
year. These schools are traditional public schools that fall below certain performance and
poverty thresholds. Students assigned to them are eligible for an EdChoice scholarship to
attend a participating chartered nonpublic school. As the chart below shows, the number of
schools designated for the 2026-2027 school year (FY 2027) increased by a net of 19 schools
over the 494 schools designated for FY 2026, with ten more districts having at least one building
designated.

On the other hand, 19 buildings came off the list. Of these, 18 buildings were no longer subject

have had buildings added for FY 2027, while one district with a FY 2026 designated building

scholarships may be awarded to students assigned to buildings that drop off the list, though
existing scholarship students assigned to these buildings generally may renew them through
grade 12.
        The amount of a traditional EdChoice scholarship in FY 2026 and FY 2027 is the lesser of
a student’s tuition and $6,166 for students in grades K-8 and $8,408 for students in grades 9-12.
Traditional EdChoice scholarships are not means-tested, unlike EdChoice expansion
scholarships. As of December 2025, approximately 42,000 students are receiving traditional
EdChoice scholarships for FY 2026, a 1.5% decrease from FY 2025. These figures may change
throughout the school year as additional students apply for and receive scholarships under the
program’s year-round application window. Traditional EdChoice scholarships for current
participants in FY 2026 are estimated to total roughly $280 million and are supported by the
GRF.
