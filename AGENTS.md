# Working in this repository

This is a yidam-derived repository: a corpus of Ohio school-funding knowledge in
`.yidam/corpus/`, a Rust workspace in `crates/` that computes against it, and an Astro site
in `web/` derived from both. The git history is the knowledge graph — every commit is an
event and the closed verb vocabulary in
[`.yidam/.vendor/prelude/GRAPH.md`](.yidam/.vendor/prelude/GRAPH.md) is what makes it
readable.

Start with [`README.md`](README.md) for what the repository holds, and
[`.yidam/.vendor/prelude/`](.yidam/.vendor/prelude/) for the conventions it inherited.
`mise run status` reports the corpus's current shape; `mise run //:ci` is the gate.

## What this repository's work is aimed at

The block below is generated by `yidam kuten` from
[`.yidam/decisions/kuten.yml`](.yidam/decisions/kuten.yml) and the profile vendored at
[`.yidam/.vendor/prelude/kuten/inquiry/`](.yidam/.vendor/prelude/kuten/inquiry/). It is the
declaration a session reads at the start rather than one it has to infer from the corpus.
It binds nobody: divergence from it is a question for a person, and this repository's
answers to the ones it currently raises are in the decision record.

**This corpus's practice is `inquiry`, at revision 2:** the corpus grows through sustained inquiry — questions opened, and settled.

- **Phases** — Investigation, Extraction, Synthesis, Assessment. Between 12% and 27% of commits settle one.
- **Vocabulary** — 30 verbs, and between 0% and 2% of commits outside them.
- **Object** — the corpus is authored in git, so its history is the record.
- **Questions** — this practice presses toward epistemic ones. It creates the pressure and authors nothing.
- **Contribution** — a session's work is read against `register` (of the commits whose verb the vocabulary recognizes, how many carry testimony rather than housekeeping), `landing` (of the nodes this range added and left standing, how many something points at) and `questions` (how many of those nodes are open questions — a number, never a threshold). `yidam score <range>` reports one row each, with the evidence, and no overall number.

It narrows the loop and may not widen the model, and it binds nobody: divergence from it is a question for a person, not a defect. Ask `yidam kuten check`.
