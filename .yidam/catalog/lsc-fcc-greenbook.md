# LSC Greenbook — Ohio Facilities Construction Commission

**Source.** Ohio Legislative Service Commission, Legislative Budget Office. *Greenbook: LBO
Analysis of Enacted Budget, Ohio Facilities Construction Commission*, H.B. 96 of the 136th
General Assembly, August 2025.
**Type.** Primary source for legislative practice. It is the legislature's own budget office
explaining the enacted appropriations and the law changes beside them.
**Location.** `lsc.ohio.gov/assets/legislation/136/hb96/en0/files/`,
`hb96-fcc-greenbook-as-enacted-136th-general-assembly.pdf`.

**What it contains.** The Commission's operating line items, FY2022 to FY2027, and the law changes
H.B. 96 made to the school facilities programs. Each line is given as actuals through FY2025 and
appropriations for the biennium, with the analyst's prose beside it.

**What it settled.** Two H.B. 96 changes to the Classroom Facilities Assistance Program, each
stated in the Commission's analyst's words:

- **The net-indebtedness alternative is gone.** Before the act, a district's local share was the
  greater of its required percentage of the basic project cost and its required level of
  indebtedness. "The budget eliminates the latter method of determining a school district's local
  share." The vendored R.C. 3318.032 carries the act's effective date of 30 September 2025 and
  has no "greater of" in division (A).
- **ALI 230647, Project Support, is new and one-time.** $20,000,000 in FY2026 and nothing in
  FY2027, with the unused balance reappropriated. It is for a district with buildings in a
  county of more than 100,000 people and in another of fewer than 50,000, whose project was
  deferred or lapsed, for a building constructed before 30 June 1925. The district must apply
  by 31 December 2025, and the state share must be at least ninety per cent.

**What it is not.** Not the capital budget. The school facilities programs' construction money
is appropriated in the capital acts as two lump lines (see
[`the-capital-channel-has-no-second-publisher`](../decisions/the-capital-channel-has-no-second-publisher.yml)),
and an operating-budget greenbook sizes only the Commission's operating lines and this one
special appropriation.
