# United States public laws — the enrolled federal acts

**Source.** U.S. Government Publishing Office, GovInfo, the *Public and Private Laws* collection.
**Type.** Primary source — the enrolled act as published in the Statutes at Large.
**Status.** *Read by hand, not vendored.* No connector fetches these, and no fixture holds their
text. The two sections below were read from GovInfo's HTML rendering of each law in October 2026.
**Location.** `www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-{congress}publ{number}/html/PLAW-{congress}publ{number}.htm`.
`www.congress.gov` and `www.ed.gov` refuse an unattended request with a 403. GovInfo serves the
same text with an ordinary browser user agent.

**What it contains.** The whole act. A pandemic relief act runs to thousands of pages, and the
sections read here are a few hundred lines each.

- **Pub. L. 116-260, division M, title III, section 312(d).** The Coronavirus Response and Relief
  Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2021, which created Emergency Assistance to Non-Public
  Schools. It reserves $2,750,000,000 of the Governor's Emergency Education Relief Fund for the
  program. It allots each state's share by "the number of children aged 5 through 17 at or below
  185 percent of poverty who are enrolled in non-public schools in the State", and has the
  Governor "designate the State educational agency to administer the program". It requires that
  "the control of funds for the services or assistance provided to a non-public school … shall be
  in a public agency". Paragraph (4)(M) permits reimbursement of some expenses a school had
  already incurred.
- **Pub. L. 117-2, section 2002.** The American Rescue Plan Act of 2021, which funded a second
  round of the program. It appropriates another $2,750,000,000, available through September 30,
  2023, for "services or assistance to non-public schools that enroll a significant percentage of
  low-income students and are most impacted by the qualifying emergency". Subsection (b) provides
  that the funds "shall not be used to provide reimbursements to any non-public school".

**Access constraints.** Freely available. The HTML keeps the Statutes at Large page breaks as
`[[Page 134 STAT. 1925]]` markers, and its editorial notes appear inline as `<<NOTE: …>>`.

**Caveats.**

- **An act states the law as enacted, not as amended.** Neither section has been checked for
  later amendment. Both programs have closed, so the enacted text is the text that governed them.
- **The federal award to Ohio is not here.** These sections set the national total and the
  formula. Ohio's allotment under each round is in the Department of Education's award notices,
  which no source here retrieves.
